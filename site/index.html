Where Malta Feels Like Home

Nestled in the honey-gold limestone streets of Cospicua, Roel Boutique B&B is your invitation to experience Malta the way it's meant to be felt — personal, warm, and authentic. Our lovingly converted townhouses blend original Maltese character with modern comforts, and our host Ledia is here to make sure your stay feels less like a hotel visit and more like coming home to a friend's beautiful house in the Mediterranean.

Top rated on Booking.com and Google, Roel is one of Malta's highest-rated accommodations — and we believe that's because we treat every guest as a person, not a room number.

Your Stay at Roel

Every stay comes with the personal touches that make Roel special. From a freshly prepared breakfast each morning to insider tips from your host Ledia, we take care of the details so you can focus on enjoying Malta.

Stay in a lovingly converted townhouse of honey-gold Maltese limestone, moments from the Grand Harbour and the historic streets of the Three Cities.

Whether you're exploring the ancient fortifications of the Three Cities, catching the ferry to Valletta, or simply unwinding on our rooftop terrace with a glass of local wine — Roel is the perfect base for your Maltese adventure.

Our Legacy

Our Story in Stone

Set in the honey-gold limestone streets of Cospicua, Roel was created from two townhouses lovingly converted into a boutique B&B — with one original entrance door kept just as it was. Every room blends authentic Maltese character with the modern comforts you need for a perfect stay.

Our Neighbourhood

Malta's Best-Kept Secret

While most visitors flock to Valletta and Sliema, the Three Cities — Cospicua, Birgu, and Senglea — are where Malta's real story unfolds. Quiet waterfront promenades, centuries of history around every corner, and the authentic rhythm of Maltese daily life. Step outside Roel and you're in the heart of it.

Learn More
Perfectly Connected

Ten Minutes to the Capital

A scenic ferry ride across the Grand Harbour — just ten minutes — and you're in Valletta, Malta's UNESCO World Heritage capital. St John's Co-Cathedral, Upper Barrakka Gardens, and world-class restaurants are all within walking distance of the ferry terminal. Explore the capital by day, come home to Roel's peaceful streets at night.
